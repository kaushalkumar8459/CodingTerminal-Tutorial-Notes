## Source Accuracy Rule

Every question should belong to one of these classifications:

### Known / Documented

A public company guide or attributed candidate report explicitly supports the interview question/pattern.

### Company-Oriented

The exercise is derived from a company's public product and engineering themes.

### General Practice

The exercise is useful preparation but does not have company-specific evidence.

Never state that a question was asked by a company unless the source supports it.
